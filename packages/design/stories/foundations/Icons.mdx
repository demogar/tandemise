# Icons

Stroke icons on a 24px grid at a 1.7 weight, drawn in `currentColor` so they
take the colour of their text. Draw them with `<Icon name="…" />` from
`@tandemise/design/react`, or from `ICON_PATHS` anywhere else. Add a glyph in
`src/icons.ts`.
