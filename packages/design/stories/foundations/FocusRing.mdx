# Focus ring

Every focusable element shows the same ring on `:focus-visible` (`base.css`): a
`--focus-ring-width` outline in `--focus-ring`, offset by `--focus-ring-offset`.
The colour holds at least 3:1 against every surface in both themes (WCAG
1.4.11). Tab through the controls below.
