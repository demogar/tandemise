# Logo

Two wheels on one axle: a tandem. It also reads as two nodes on an edge, one
piece of work handed from one worker to the next. The lead wheel is larger, so
the mark has a direction of travel.

## In the app

`<Logo />` draws the mark in the `--logo-*` roles, so it follows the theme.
`muted` means "nothing to connect to" (the daemon is down), never decoration.
Minimum size 16px; clear space at least half its height.

<LogoVariants />

## Assets

Emitted from `LOGO` into `assets/`. `logo-mark.svg` follows the viewer's scheme;
the template is pure black for the macOS menu bar; the app icon is the same mark
redrawn for 1024px on an indigo tile.

<LogoAssets />

## Don'ts

- Do not make the wheels touch (an infinity sign) or the same size (spectacles).
- Do not run the axle through the wheels, or add a hub (a blob at 16px).
- Do not recolour it outside the palette.
